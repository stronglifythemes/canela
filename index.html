Got Questions?

Do you offer gluten-free options?

While we specialize in traditional wheat baguettes, we do offer our fillings as "Bánh Mì Bowls" over rice noodles or salad for a gluten-friendly alternative. Please note our kitchen handles wheat flour.

Can I pre-order for a large group?

Absolutely! We require 24 hours notice for orders over 15 rolls. You can place catering orders directly through our website or give us a call.

Are your ingredients sourced locally?

Yes, we pride ourselves on using fresh produce from Canterbury suppliers wherever possible, combined with authentic imported spices to ensure the genuine Northern Vietnamese flavor.
